A SECOND USER, AND WHAT A POLICY DOES (chapter 6).

    POST /Users/New  {"Name":"guest","Password":"guest-view-42"}
    -> HTTP 200, new user id 302f2ae83dde46fc871789f13aae030c

A new user starts out seeing everything the admin sees.
Signed in as guest, a recursive listing of the library:

    guest sees 12 items — both movies, the album and its tracks,
    the series, its season, both episodes, three library folders.

Restricting the account: in 12.2 the policy is read from the
user object (GET /Users/<id> -> "Policy"), edited, and posted
back to POST /Users/<id>/Policy. The two fields that matter:

    "EnableAllFolders": false,
    "EnabledFolders": ["<the Movies library's id>"]

    POST /Users/<guest>/Policy  -> HTTP 204

The same guest listing afterwards:

    guest sees 3 items
      Movie  | The Clockwork Lighthouse
      Movie  | A Field Guide to Clouds
      Folder | Movies

Music and Series are not greyed out or locked — to this user
they do not exist. That absence is the access control working
as intended, and it is per-user: the admin's view is unchanged.

THE SERVER'S OWN PROBE OF A SPECIMEN (chapter 5).
GET /Users/<admin>/Items/<the Clockwork Lighthouse item>:

    Name: The Clockwork Lighthouse | Year: 2021
    RuntimeTicks: 60000000            (= 6.0 seconds)
     stream: Video h264 1280x720 3010332 bits/s
     stream: Audio aac 69447 bits/s

The year came from the "(2021)" in the filename — the file has
no year tag inside it. The stream figures agree with ffprobe's
independent reading of the same file (transcript t04): two
probes, one file, same numbers. This probing is what Jellyfin
does to every file in every library on every scan; it is how
the server knows what a client can be sent directly and what
must be transcoded (chapter 7).

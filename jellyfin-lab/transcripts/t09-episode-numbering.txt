EPISODE NUMBERS, AND THE SCAN THAT LOOKED BROKEN (chapter 5).

Half an hour into the lab, the server's database said this
about the two episodes (SQLite, data/data/jellyfin.db):

    SELECT Name, IndexNumber, ParentIndexNumber FROM BaseItems
    WHERE Type LIKE '%Episode%';
    North Pier S01E01 - Foghorn|          |
    North Pier - S01E02 - Low Water|      |

No season number, no episode number — with a Series ("North
Pier") and a Season already created around them. The natural
conclusion (the naming is wrong) was wrong. The server log
told the real story: every scan so far had been interrupted.

    "Scan Media Library" Cancelled after 1 minute(s) and 6 seconds
    ... "Scan Media Library" Cancelled after 2 minute(s) and 0 seconds

Each new "scan now" request cancels the scan in progress, and
the realtime file monitor had queued more; the final phases of
a scan — where episodes get their numbers — never got to run.

One full, uninterrupted scan later, the same query:

    North Pier S01E01 - Foghorn|1|1
    North Pier - S01E02 - Low Water|2|1

Both filename shapes parse: "North Pier S01E01 - Foghorn" and
"North Pier - S01E02 - Low Water" are both S1 episodes 1 and 2.
Lesson kept for chapter 12: when a fresh library looks
half-linked, check whether a scan actually FINISHED
(Dashboard -> Scheduled Tasks, or the log) before blaming
your filenames.
